# Lesson 1: Equity Markets

## Learning objectives

By the end, you  should be able t explain what a stock represents and distringuish a primary market from a secondary market

## Core concepts

### What is equity?

Equity represents ownership in a company. A share is a unit of that ownership. Owning shares may provide voting rights or dividends, depending on the company and share class. A dividend is not guaranteed.

### Primary market

A company issues shares to raise capital. Investors buy newly issued shares, such as during an intial public offering (IPO)

### Secondary market

Investors trade existing shares wih one another through exchanges or other trading venues. In a typical secondary-market trade, the company is not directly receiving the trade proceeds.

### Why do a share price move ?

Prices change as buyers and sellers update the price at which they are willing to trade. Their decision may reflect company results, economic conditions, news, expectations, or other factors


## Small example

A company issues shares in an IPO. Later, an investor buys one of those shares from another investor on an exchange. The IPO is a primary-market transaction; the later investor-to-investor trade is a secondary-market transaction.
